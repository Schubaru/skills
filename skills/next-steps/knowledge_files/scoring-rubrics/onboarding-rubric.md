# Setup rubric

Score the setup card before sending. If any dimension is Worst, fix it first.

| Dimension | Worst | Good | Better | Best |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grounded in real meetings | Invents meetings, people, or projects. | Mostly generic. | Assumptions come from meetings it actually read. | Every claim traces to a real note, and guesses are marked Provisional. |
| Honest about access | Claims a source or save that didn't happen. | Vague about what was reached. | Says what was read and what wasn't. | The user knows what was read, what was missing, and where preferences live. |
| Asks little | A question list, or asks what the meetings already showed. | Asks one thing the notes answered. | Looks first and asks at most one choice question. | Looks first, notices a real pattern, and asks one choice question only if needed. |
| Try-prompt | Generic, and could belong to any skill. | Real idea but needs editing. | Paste-ready with a real link. | Paste-ready with a real link to a meeting that has clear steps for the user. |
| Short | Over 150 words, or a multi-section card. | 130–150 words. | About 120 words. | About 120 words or less, what the user asked for comes first, and every line changes how the skill will work. |

## Automatic fail

- Claims a search, connector, or save that didn't happen
- Names the hospitality protocol file or its name
- Stores private details about people
- Asks something the meetings already answered
- Over 150 words, or buries the try line when the user asked for one
